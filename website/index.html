No Pinhole Proxies

Measurement-consistent Gaussian splatting for fisheye–LiDAR rigs不用针孔替身：面向鱼眼–激光雷达设备的测量一致高斯泼溅

TL;DR

3D Gaussian models of scanned spaces look photoreal but measure poorly, even though the LiDAR on the same rig measured the geometry precisely. We trace this to how the two sensors are taught to the model: each is replaced by a stand-in pinhole camera, and the two stand-ins disagree about what “depth” means. Our proposal has three parts: treat every fisheye pixel and every laser beam as the same thing, a ray, so both sensors follow one rule; let the laser’s measurements decide where image fitting may move a surface; and do it inside the existing fast trainer without slowing it down. All three are hypotheses under test. No result is shown yet.

用扫描设备重建的 3D 高斯模型看起来很真，量起来却不准，哪怕同一台设备上的激光雷达已经把几何量得很精确。我们认为问题出在“怎么教”：两种传感器各自被换成一个针孔相机“替身”，而两个替身对“深度”的定义并不一致。我们的方案分三步：把每个鱼眼像素和每条激光束都当成同一种东西——射线，让两种传感器遵守同一条规则；让激光的测量决定图像拟合可以在哪里挪动表面；并且全部放进现有的高速训练器，不拖慢训练。三者都是待验证的假设，页面上还没有任何结果。

01The gap鸿沟

Looks right, measures wrong看起来对，量起来不对

Handheld scanners, backpack and vehicle mapping systems and 360° rigs all pair wide-angle cameras with a LiDAR. Their users want a digital twin that is both: something you can walk through, and something you can put a tape measure on.

手持扫描仪、背包和车载测绘系统、360° 采集设备，都把广角相机和激光雷达装在一起。用户想要的数字孪生要同时做到两件事：能在里面“走”，也能拿尺子去“量”。

Gaussian splatting [1] solved the first half. The second half lags behind. On Oxford Spires [2], where a terrestrial laser scanner (TLS) provides independent ground truth at 1 cm, the official LiDAR maps reach an F-score at 10 cm of 0.54–0.85, while a radiance field trained on the same captures reaches 0.31–0.68.

高斯泼溅 [1] 解决了前一半，后一半还落在后面。在 Oxford Spires [2] 上，地面激光扫描仪（TLS）提供 1 cm 精度的独立真值：官方激光地图的 10 cm F 分数是 0.54–0.85，而用同样数据训练的辐射场只有 0.31–0.68。

The camera side has recently been fixed: native fisheye renderers [3] [4] [5] [6] stopped cutting the fisheye into small perspective images, which threw away field of view and resolution. That leaves an unavoidable question: once the camera is handled natively and the LiDAR is added as depth supervision, does the geometric gap close?

相机这一侧最近已经补上了：原生鱼眼渲染器 [3] [4] [5] [6] 不再把鱼眼图切成小块透视图，不再白白丢掉视场和分辨率。于是绕不开一个问题：相机已经原生处理、激光也作为深度监督加进来之后，几何上的差距会不会消失？

Key takeaway要点

We do not know yet, and the plan is built around that. Experiment 1 measures it first. If native-fisheye splatting with LiDAR supervision already matches the very scans it was trained on, there is no gap to explain, and the rest of this page is withdrawn.

我们还不知道，整个计划正是围绕这一点设计的。实验一最先测它。如果“原生鱼眼 + 激光监督”的高斯泼溅已经追平了它吃进去的激光扫描，就没有鸿沟需要解释，本页其余内容随之撤回。

02Why成因

Two stand-ins that disagree about depth两个替身，对“深度”各说各话

Robot mapping has always treated a sensor reading as a measurement: there is a model of how the sensor works, and the map must agree with it [10] [11]. Radiance fields treat images as pictures to reproduce [12] [1]. With one camera the two views are the same thing. Add a LiDAR and they part ways: inverse rendering is not inverse measurement.

机器人建图一直把传感器读数当作测量：有一个描述传感器如何工作的模型，地图必须与之相符 [10] [11]。辐射场则把图像当作要复现的画面 [12] [1]。只有一台相机时，两种看法是一回事；加上激光雷达，它们就分道扬镳了：逆渲染不等于逆测量。

The usual way to bring a LiDAR into a renderer is to dress it up as another camera. Our own baseline, R1 (the working system of §09), is a typical case. It cuts each fisheye image into perspective crops, rendered with the affine EWA approximation [8] whose error grows away from the image centre [9], and replaces the LiDAR with a virtual pinhole depth camera (120° wide, 256 × 256 queries) that renders

把激光雷达接进渲染器，常见做法是把它“打扮”成另一台相机。我们自己的基线 R1（§09 那套能跑的系统）就是典型：把每张鱼眼图切成透视小图（用仿射近似的 EWA 投影渲染 [8]，越往边缘误差越大 [9]），再把激光雷达换成一台虚拟的针孔深度相机（视场 120°，256 × 256 个查询），它渲染的是

D_{\mathrm{R1}}(q) \;=\; \sum_i \; z_i\;\alpha_i\,\mathcal G_i(q)\;T_i(q)

In words: an opacity-weighted average of the depths of Gaussian centres. z_i is the depth of Gaussian i’s centre, \alpha_i\mathcal G_i(q) how much of its footprint covers the query, T_i(q) how much light is left in front of it.

用大白话说：高斯中心深度的加权平均。z_i 是第 i 个高斯中心的深度，\alpha_i\mathcal G_i(q) 是它的投影覆盖了这个查询多少，T_i(q) 是它前面还剩多少光。

i · What is measuredi · 量的是什么

Centre, not distance中心，而不是距离

A 50 cm Gaussian lying at a slant on a wall reports its centre depth to every beam that crosses its edge. The laser measured the distance along its own beam.

一个 50 cm 的高斯斜贴在墙上，凡是擦过它边缘的光束，它都报自己中心的深度；而激光量的是沿自己光束的距离。

ii · Which statisticii · 用哪个统计量

Mean, median, first hit均值、中位、首次命中

Three rules coexist. A faint floater in front of a wall drags the average into empty air long before the median notices.

三条规则同时存在。墙前一团淡淡的漂浮物，就能把平均值拽进空气里，而中位值还毫无察觉。

iii · Which directioniii · 往哪个方向改

No push along the beam不能沿光束推

Geometry can only change by fading Gaussians or sliding them sideways, never by moving one closer or farther along the line of sight.

几何只能靠让高斯变淡或侧向滑动来改变，永远不能沿视线把它推近或推远。

What to watch看点

  1. Raise the floater’s opacity. The baseline’s average slides into empty space while the measured return stays put.
  2. Tilt the wall. The beam now meets the Gaussian away from its centre, and the stand-in keeps reporting the centre.
  3. Push the floater past one half. Every median, ours included, jumps to the floater; catching this is what the empty-space term and the “blocking” label are for.
  1. 调高漂浮物的不透明度：基线的平均深度滑进了空气里，实测回波却纹丝不动。
  2. 倾斜墙面：光束在偏离中心的地方碰到高斯，替身仍然报中心深度。
  3. 让漂浮物超过一半不透明：所有中位深度（包括我们的）都会跳到漂浮物上。把它揪出来，正是“空域项”和“挡路”标签的用处。

Key takeaway要点

The shared Gaussians are given contradictory targets, and the cheapest way for the optimiser to satisfy everyone is to let appearance absorb the conflict. That is our explanation of the gap, and it can be wrong: if replacing the stand-ins changes nothing (Experiment 2), the explanation fails.

同一批高斯收到了互相矛盾的目标，优化器最省事的办法，就是让外观把矛盾吸收掉。这是我们对鸿沟的解释，而且它可能是错的：如果把替身换掉之后什么都没变（实验二），这个解释就不成立。

04Contribution 1 · One ray model贡献 1 · 统一射线模型

A pixel and a laser beam are the same thing: a ray像素和激光束本是同一种东西：射线

In one sentence. Stop dressing the sensors up as pinhole cameras. A fisheye pixel is a ray leaving the camera; a laser return is a ray leaving the scanner. Render both the same way, and let both stop at the same place.

一句话。别再把传感器打扮成针孔相机。一个鱼眼像素是从相机出发的一条射线，一个激光回波是从扫描仪出发的一条射线。用同一种方式渲染它们，并让它们停在同一个地方。

\begin{aligned}\text{camera pixel } u:&\quad \bo=\text{camera centre},\quad \bd = R_{w\leftarrow c}\,\pi_c^{-1}(u)\\ \text{LiDAR return } k:&\quad \bo_k=\text{origin at the return's own time},\quad \bd_k=\text{beam},\quad \rho_k=\text{range}\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}\text{相机像素 } u:&\quad \bo=\text{相机中心},\quad \bd = R_{w\leftarrow c}\,\pi_c^{-1}(u)\\ \text{激光回波 } k:&\quad \bo_k=\text{该回波时刻的发射原点},\quad \bd_k=\text{光束方向},\quad \rho_k=\text{测距}\end{aligned}

\pi_c is the calibrated fisheye model. The whole image circle is used at native resolution, never resampled; there is no crop and no virtual depth camera.

\pi_c 是标定好的鱼眼模型。整个成像圆以原生分辨率使用，不重采样；没有裁切，也没有虚拟深度相机。

Where a ray meets a Gaussian射线在哪里遇到一个高斯

\begin{gathered}\tau_i^*=\frac{\bd^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}(\bmu_i-\bo)}{\bd^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}\bd}\\[6pt] D_i^2=(\bmu_i-\bo)^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}(\bmu_i-\bo)-\tau_i^{*2}\,\bd^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}\bd,\qquad a_i=\alpha_i\,e^{-\frac12 D_i^2}\end{gathered}

Read it like this这样读

Stretch space until the Gaussian becomes a round ball (that is what \Sigma_i^{-1} does). Straight lines stay straight, so the ray is still a ray. Now \tau_i^* is simply the point on the ray closest to the ball’s centre, and D_i is how far it misses. Close miss, high opacity a_i; wide miss, almost none.

No projection, no approximation. This is the peak response of 3DGRT [7]; 3DGEER [6] shows it equals the exact integral of the Gaussian along the ray.

把空间拉伸，直到这个高斯变成一个圆球（\Sigma_i^{-1} 做的就是这件事）。直线拉伸后还是直线，所以射线还是射线。这时 \tau_i^* 就是射线上离球心最近的那一点，D_i 是射线偏离球心多远。擦得近，不透明度 a_i 就高；偏得远，几乎为零。

没有投影，也没有近似。这就是 3DGRT [7] 的峰值响应；3DGEER [6] 证明它恰好等于高斯沿射线的精确积分。

What to watch看点

  1. Slide the ray across the Gaussian. The stand-in’s centre depth never moves; \tau^* follows the surface.
  2. Drag the morph to “whitened”. The ellipse becomes a circle and \tau^* becomes an ordinary closest point.
  3. Rotate the Gaussian to lie obliquely to the ray: that is where centre depth and true distance disagree most.
  1. 让射线横扫过高斯：替身报的中心深度一动不动，\tau^* 却跟着表面走。
  2. 把变形滑块拖到“白化”：椭圆变成圆，\tau^* 变成普通的最近点。
  3. 把高斯转到与射线斜交：这正是中心深度和真实距离差得最多的情形。

One stopping depth for both sensors两种传感器，同一个停止深度

T_i=\prod_{j\lt i}(1-a_j),\qquad k(\mathbf r):\;T_{k+1}\le\tfrac12\lt T_k,\qquad \tau_{\mathrm{term}}(\mathbf r)=\tau^*_{k(\mathbf r)}

Walk down the ray, keep track of how much light is left (T), and stop at the first Gaussian after which less than half remains. Where it peaks is the ray’s stopping depth \tau_{\mathrm{term}}. A camera pixel and a laser beam both use this one number.

沿射线往前走，记下还剩多少光（T），在剩余光第一次少于一半的那个高斯处停下。它的峰值位置就是这条射线的停止深度 \tau_{\mathrm{term}}。相机像素和激光束都用这同一个数。

Credit. This is the median depth of 2DGS [18] and GS-LiDAR [19], also used by SplatAD at inference [13] and formalised by Geometry-Grounded GS [20]. We do not claim it. What is new is its role: the camera’s depth, the laser’s predicted distance, the surface used for evaluation and the bookkeeping of §05 become one and the same number.

归属。这就是 2DGS [18] 和 GS-LiDAR [19] 的中位深度，SplatAD 推理时也用 [13]，Geometry-Grounded GS 给出了形式化 [20]。我们不主张这个定义。新的是它的角色：相机的深度、激光的预测距离、评估用的表面、§05 的记账，变成了同一个数。

A push along the beam一股沿光束的推力

\frac{\partial\tau_k^*}{\partial\bmu_k}=\frac{\Sigma_k^{-1}\bd}{\bd^\top\Sigma_k^{-1}\bd}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \bd^\top\frac{\partial\tau_k^*}{\partial\bmu_k}=1

The right-hand identity is the intuition: slide the stopping Gaussian one centimetre along the beam and the stopping depth slides one centimetre. A distance error can finally move geometry along the line of sight, which the baseline could not do (mismatch iii).

右边这个恒等式就是直觉：把停止处的高斯沿光束挪 1 cm，停止深度也挪 1 cm。距离误差终于能沿视线方向推动几何了，这正是基线做不到的（不一致 iii）。

What the laser is asked to explain要求激光解释什么

\mathcal L_{\mathrm{lidar}}=\underbrace{\psi_\delta\big(\tau_{\mathrm{term}}-\rho\big)}_{\text{stop where it returned}}\;+\;\lambda_{\mathrm{fs}}\underbrace{\big(1-T(\rho-\kappa\sigma_\rho)\big)}_{\text{empty space before the return}}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{lidar}}=\underbrace{\psi_\delta\big(\tau_{\mathrm{term}}-\rho\big)}_{\text{在回波处停下}}\;+\;\lambda_{\mathrm{fs}}\underbrace{\big(1-T(\rho-\kappa\sigma_\rho)\big)}_{\text{回波之前是空的}}

The first term (a robust Huber penalty) asks the beam to stop where it actually returned. The second asks the space the laser just flew through to be empty, up to the sensor’s noise \kappa\sigma_\rho; this is the line-of-sight idea of Urban Radiance Fields [22]. The camera keeps its usual colour loss and needs no depth loss of its own: the laser already shapes the surface the camera sees.

第一项（稳健的 Huber 惩罚）要求光束停在它真正返回的地方。第二项要求激光刚刚穿过的那段空间是空的（留出传感器噪声 \kappa\sigma_\rho 的余量），这是 Urban Radiance Fields [22] 的视线思想。相机保留通常的颜色损失，不再需要自己的深度损失：相机看到的表面，已经由激光塑造好了。

The claim, stated so that it can fail主张，写成可以被否定的样子

With one set of Gaussians and one measurement rule, the result should move off the photo-versus-geometry trade-off curve that loss weighting traces, not just to another point on it. If it lands on the curve, SimULi’s choice to split the scene is the better one, and C1 is reported as an implementation detail. 一套高斯、一条测量规则，结果应当跳出损失加权画出的“画质–几何”此消彼长曲线，而不只是落到曲线上的另一个点。如果落在曲线上，那 SimULi 拆分场景的选择更好，C1 就只算实现细节。两种传感器是否该共用同一个“一半”阈值，也一并检验（0.3、0.5、0.7）。

05Contribution 2 · Laser-anchored optimisation贡献 2 · 激光锚定优化

Where the laser has measured, images may not move the surface激光量过的地方，图像不能把表面挪走

In one sentence. Do not argue with a measurement by adding yet another weighted loss. Where the laser has seen a surface, let images slide it along but not pull it off; where the laser saw nothing, let images do as they please.

一句话。不要靠再加一个带权重的损失去和测量讨价还价。激光看到过表面的地方，图像可以让它沿表面滑动，但不能把它拉离；激光没看到的地方，图像想怎么改就怎么改。

The standard way to give a radiance field better geometry is to add a loss with a weight: distortion [24], normal and planarity terms [18] [23], depth terms [21] [22]. A weight only chooses a point on a trade-off, for a structural reason:

让辐射场几何更好，标准做法是加一个带权重的损失：distortion [24]、法向与平面项 [18] [23]、深度项 [21] [22]。权重只能在此消彼长的曲线上挑一个点，这有结构上的原因：

\min_\theta\;\; f_{\mathrm{rgb}}(\theta)\;+\;\lambda\,f_{\mathrm{geo}}(\theta)

For a fixed weight \lambda, the answer is where a straight line of slope -\lambda first touches the set of achievable models. Sweeping \lambda rolls the line around that set, so it can only ever touch its convex outline; any stretch of the best-possible front that bulges outward is unreachable at every weight [25]. A constraint (f_{\mathrm{geo}}\le\varepsilon) has no such blind spot. And a loss never takes a direction away: images can still buy appearance by moving a measured surface.

权重 \lambda 固定时，解落在斜率为 -\lambda 的直线第一次碰到“可达模型集合”的地方。扫一遍 \lambda，就是让这条直线绕着集合滚一圈，所以它只能碰到集合的凸轮廓；最优前沿上任何向外鼓出的一段，不管权重取多少都够不到 [25]。约束（f_{\mathrm{geo}}\le\varepsilon）没有这个盲区。而且损失从不拿走任何一个方向：图像依然可以靠挪动一个已测表面来换取更好的画面。

Bookkeeping along the same rays沿同一批射线记账

Because both sensors now reach the same Gaussians through the same stopping depth, the laser pass can keep a simple record for every Gaussian. For each beam with a return \rho:

两种传感器现在通过同一个停止深度落到同一批高斯上，所以激光那一趟渲染可以顺手给每个高斯记一本简单的账。对每条有回波 \rho 的光束：

\begin{aligned} h_i &\mathrel{+}= 1 && \text{if } i=k(\mathbf r)\ \text{and}\ |\tau_i^*-\rho|\le\kappa\sigma_\rho\\ f_i &\mathrel{+}= a_iT_i && \text{if } \tau_i^*\lt\rho-\kappa\sigma_\rho\\ n_i &\mathrel{+}= 1 && \text{if } T_i\gt\epsilon \end{aligned}
  • Hit h_i: this Gaussian is where the beam stopped and returned.
  • Blocked f_i: it sits in space the beam flew through, weighted by how much light it took.
  • Seen n_i: the beam reached it at all.
  • 命中 h_i：光束就停在这个高斯上并返回。
  • 挡路 f_i：它待在光束飞过的空间里，按它挡掉的光加权。
  • 照到 n_i：光束到达过它。

Every 100 steps a running average of the three counts gives each Gaussian one of three labels:

每 100 步，用三个计数的滑动平均给每个高斯贴上三种标签之一：

◆Anchored锚定n_i\ge n_{\min}\ \wedge\ h_i/n_i\ge\eta_hmeasured, and it is the surface被测到过，而且它就是表面
✕Blocking挡路f_i/n_i\ge\eta_f\ \wedge\ h_i/n_i\lt\eta_hsits where the laser saw empty space待在激光证明为空的地方
○Unseen未测到n_i\lt n_{\min}the laser is silent here激光在这里没有发言权

The anchoring rule锚定规则

\Delta\bmu_i^{\mathrm{rgb}}\;\leftarrow\;\big(I-\bn_i\bn_i^{\top}\big)\,\Delta\bmu_i^{\mathrm{rgb}},\qquad s_{i,\min}\le\sigma_\rho

For an anchored Gaussian with surface normal \bn_i, the image-driven move \Delta\bmu_i^{\mathrm{rgb}} keeps only its part along the surface; I-\bn\bn^\top is exactly “drop the part through the surface”. The laser’s own update is never touched. Its thickness is held to the sensor noise (\sigma_\rho\approx 3 cm for the Hesai QT64) and it is split only within the surface. A blocking Gaussian fades and is removed, never copied. An unseen Gaussian is optimised exactly as in 3DGS.

对一个法向为 \bn_i 的锚定高斯，图像驱动的位移 \Delta\bmu_i^{\mathrm{rgb}} 只保留沿表面的部分；I-\bn\bn^\top 做的恰好就是“去掉穿过表面的那部分”。激光自己的更新从不被动。它的厚度被压在传感器噪声以内（Hesai QT64 的 \sigma_\rho\approx 3 cm），分裂也只在表面内进行。挡路的高斯逐渐变淡并被删除，绝不复制。未测到的高斯完全按 3DGS 照常优化。

Key takeaway要点

No weight to tune. One direction is taken away from image fitting where, and only where, the laser measured it. This only works because the stopping depth is shared: without C1 the record would mix three different notions of depth.

没有权重要调。只在激光量过的地方、只拿走被量过的那一个方向。这之所以成立，是因为停止深度是共用的：没有 C1，这本账会混进三种不同的“深度”。

Falsifiable prediction可证伪的预言

In places the cameras see but the laser never returns from, our completeness against the survey scan should beat EnerGS, which freezes image control of positions everywhere (§07). If it does not, C2 is no different from its closest neighbour in any way we can measure, and is reported as an engineering choice.

在相机看得到、激光却从未返回的地方，我们对照测绘扫描的完整度应当超过 EnerGS——它在所有地方都冻结了图像对位置的控制（§07）。如果没有超过，C2 与最近邻没有可测的区别，就只作为工程选择报告。

06Contribution 3 · Speed贡献 3 · 速度

More faithful, without slowing down更忠实，但不变慢

In one sentence. Keep every trick that makes the existing trainer fast, and change only the one formula that turns a Gaussian into opacity.

一句话。保留现有训练器所有让它快的手段，只换掉“把高斯变成不透明度”的那一个公式。

Our trainer already carries the rasterisation lineage’s accelerations: GPU-resident storage, tile binning with warp-cooperative duplication, radix sorting, an Adam that updates only visible Gaussians [29], fused SSIM and a warp-shuffle backward pass. C3 claims only that C1 and C2 need to remove none of them.

我们的训练器已经带着光栅化这条线的各种加速：数据常驻 GPU、按 tile 分箱并由 warp 协作复制、基数排序、只更新可见高斯的 Adam [29]、融合 SSIM、warp-shuffle 反向。C3 只主张一件事：C1 和 C2 不需要拆掉其中任何一项。

Isn’t this just ray tracing, or NeRF?这不就退化成光线追踪或 NeRF 了吗？

No. Ray tracing such as 3DGRT [7] is expensive because it searches: it builds a BVH and walks it for every ray. NeRF is expensive because it samples dozens of points per ray and runs a network at each. We do neither. Gaussians are still sorted into screen tiles exactly as in 3DGS, each pixel still visits only the Gaussians in its tile, once each; only the formula evaluated per visit changes. \Sigma^{-1}=RS^{-2}R^\top costs the same as the \Sigma that 3DGS already builds, once per Gaussian, not per pixel.

不会。3DGRT [7] 这类光线追踪贵在找：要建 BVH，每条光线都要遍历它。NeRF 贵在采样：每条射线采几十个点，每个点跑一次网络。这两件事我们都不做。高斯仍然像 3DGS 一样被分进屏幕 tile，每个像素仍然只访问自己 tile 里的高斯、每个只访问一次；变的只是每次访问时算的那个公式。\Sigma^{-1}=RS^{-2}R^\top 的代价和 3DGS 本来就要算的 \Sigma 一样，而且是每个高斯算一次，不是每个像素算一次。

The real extra costs are elsewhere, and must be measured: about 11 instead of 6 numbers per Gaussian in shared memory, about 14 instead of 9 gradient components in the backward pass, larger tile footprints near the fisheye rim, and above all more pixels. Laser returns each have their own origin, so their per-Gaussian constants are recomputed per return (a few multiply-adds) or per packet of near-identical origins.

真正的额外开销在别处，必须实测：每个高斯放进共享内存的数从约 6 个涨到约 11 个；反向的梯度分量从约 9 个涨到约 14 个；鱼眼边缘处的 tile 覆盖更大；最主要的是像素更多了。每个激光回波都有自己的原点，所以它的逐高斯常量要按回波重算（多几次乘加），或者按原点几乎相同的小包分组计算。

Why the exact response is cheap为什么精确响应不贵

\underbrace{\mathbf v_i=\Sigma_i^{-1}(\bmu_i-\bo),\quad c_i=(\bmu_i-\bo)^\top\mathbf v_i}_{\text{once per Gaussian}}\qquad \underbrace{b=\bd^\top\mathbf v_i,\quad q=\bd^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}\bd}_{\text{per pixel}}\;\Longrightarrow\;\tau_i^*=\frac bq,\quad D_i^2=c_i-\frac{b^2}{q}

Per pixel–Gaussian pair, two dot products and one division remain. With the six products d_jd_k of a pixel’s direction formed once outside the loop, q is six fused multiply-adds. What grows is the number of pixels: a native Oxford fisheye frame has about 2.9× the pixels of the official pinhole crop. We expect that, not the formula, to dominate any slowdown; it is a derivation, not a measurement.

每个“像素×高斯”对，只剩两个点积和一次除法。像素方向的六个乘积 d_jd_k 在循环外算好之后，q 就是六次乘加。真正增长的是像素数：Oxford 一帧原生鱼眼的像素约是官方针孔裁切的 2.9 倍。我们预计拖慢训练的主要是它，而不是公式；这是推算，不是测量。

Related accelerations are credited, not claimed: Taming 3DGS [29] for budgets and sparse Adam, Faster-GS [30] for the restructured backward pass, StopThePop [31] for sorting. Unlike SplatAD [13], the laser reuses the camera’s binning and formula instead of a second rasteriser.

相关加速归功于原作者，不作为我们的主张：Taming 3DGS [29]（预算与稀疏 Adam）、Faster-GS [30]（重构反向）、StopThePop [31]（排序）。与 SplatAD [13] 不同，激光复用相机的分箱和公式，而不是另写一条光栅。

Key takeaway要点

The speed claim is relative, because the trainer’s speed is inherited: reaching the baseline’s final quality should take at most 1.2× as long as the baseline on the same RTX 5090. Beyond 1.2×, the claim is dropped and the slowdown reported as an implementation detail.

速度主张是相对的，因为训练器本身的快是继承来的：在同一张 RTX 5090 上，达到基线最终质量所用的时间不超过基线的 1.2 倍。超过 1.2 倍，就放弃这条主张，如实把变慢报告为实现细节。

The three closest neighbours, in one line each三个最近邻，各一句话

SimULi · ICLR 2026

Same sensors, opposite choice同样的传感器，相反的选择

SimULi found that one set of Gaussians trades image quality against laser accuracy, and split the scene in two. We test whether that trade-off disappears once both sensors share one measurement rule. Different goal, too: dynamic driving simulation vs. static metric reconstruction.

SimULi 发现一套高斯会在画质和激光精度之间此消彼长，于是把场景拆成两套。我们检验的是：两种传感器共用同一条测量规则之后，这种此消彼长会不会消失。目标也不同：动态驾驶仿真 vs. 静态度量重建。

EnerGS · ICML 2026

Same instinct, different reach同样的直觉，不同的作用范围

EnerGS also lets LiDAR override images, but for every Gaussian position, everywhere. We remove only the one direction the laser measured, only where it measured, so places the laser cannot see can still be completed from images.

EnerGS 也让激光压过图像，但作用于所有高斯的位置、所有地方。我们只拿走激光量过的那一个方向、只在它量过的地方，所以激光看不到的地方仍然能靠图像补全。

3DGEER · DirectFisheye-GS · 3DGUT

Their camera half, our second sensor相机一半是他们的，第二个传感器是我们的

The camera half of C1 is theirs and we claim nothing there. They model one sensor and are judged on photos; we add the laser as the same kind of ray and judge geometry against survey scans.

C1 的相机那一半是他们的，我们在那里不主张任何东西。他们只建模一种传感器、只按照片评估；我们把激光作为同一种射线加进来，并用测绘扫描评估几何。

Two more works restrict an optimiser, for a different job: GeoP-Calib [27] stops image gradients to all Gaussians while calibrating camera–LiDAR extrinsics, and Peng et al. [28] constrain camera parameters with barriers. Both act globally on a different set of variables.

还有两项工作也约束优化器，但用于别的目的：GeoP-Calib [27] 在标定相机–激光外参时，阻断图像对所有高斯的梯度；Peng 等 [28] 用障碍函数约束相机参数。两者都是全局作用于另一组变量。

Explicitly not claimed: native fisheye rendering, the exact ray response, the median stopping depth, the empty-space loss, and three-state LiDAR labels. Each has a prior owner, cited above.

明确不主张：原生鱼眼渲染、精确射线响应、中位停止深度、空域损失、激光三态标签。它们都有在先的出处，均已引用。

08Validation验证

Four questions, each with the answer that would stop us四个问题，每个都写明什么结果会让我们停下

Everything is tested on one site first: Oxford Spires Keble-04 [2], to our knowledge the only public dataset with fisheye colour cameras (three 1440 × 1080), a synchronised mobile LiDAR (Hesai QT64) and independent survey geometry (TLS at 1 cm). Every method trains on one RTX 5090 for the same wall-clock time with three seeds, and is scored the same way: about 46 training runs in all.

全部先在一个场地上检验：Oxford Spires Keble-04 [2]。据我们所知，它是唯一同时具备鱼眼彩色相机（三台 1440 × 1080）、同步移动激光雷达（Hesai QT64）和独立测绘几何（1 cm TLS）的公开数据集。每个方法都在同一张 RTX 5090 上、相同墙钟时间、三个随机种子下训练，并用同一把尺子打分，总共约 46 次训练。

One ruler for every method. Geometry is extracted the same way for all splatting methods: back-project the stopping depth of every training ray, drop rays that never stop or exceed 60 m, then score accuracy, completeness and F-score at 5 and 10 cm against TLS. The input-LiDAR row is the training scans themselves. The blind region is where TLS has points but the training LiDAR has no return within 10 cm, while at least two cameras see it. Differences smaller than one seed’s standard deviation are not interpreted.

所有方法一把尺子。所有泼溅方法用同一种方式取几何：把每条训练射线的停止深度反投影，丢掉从不停止或超过 60 m 的射线，再对照 TLS 算准确度、完整度和 5 / 10 cm F 分数。输入激光一行就是训练用的扫描本身。盲区指 TLS 有点、但 10 cm 内没有任何训练激光回波，且至少两台相机看得到的地方。小于一个种子标准差的差异不做解读。

Key takeaway要点

The order is fixed because the contributions depend on each other: C2’s bookkeeping is defined by C1’s stopping depth, and C3 carries only what C1 and C2 change. If Q1 fails, nothing downstream runs.

顺序是固定的，因为三个贡献环环相扣：C2 的记账由 C1 的停止深度定义，C3 承接的也只是 C1 和 C2 的改动。Q1 不通过，后面全部不跑。

Known limitations已知局限

  • Calibration and timing. One shared set of Gaussians must absorb leftover calibration and timing errors between the sensors. SimULi blames the camera–LiDAR trade-off on exactly these errors [17]; a static handheld rig does not escape that just by being static. Sensitivity to them will be reported.
  • LiDAR physics. Beam width, multiple returns, glass and shiny surfaces break the single-stopping-point picture, and one threshold may not suit both sensors.
  • Ordering. Responses are exact per Gaussian, but the order along a ray still follows a per-Gaussian sort key.
  • Scope. Static scenes with given poses. Moving objects, sensor simulation and pose estimation are out of scope.
  • Evidence. Validation starts on one site; generality across sites, LiDARs and fisheye models is still to be shown.
  • 标定与时间同步。一套共用的高斯必须吸收传感器之间残留的标定和时间误差。SimULi 正是把相机–激光的此消彼长归因于这类误差 [17]；静态手持设备不会因为“静态”就躲过这个问题。我们会报告对这些误差的敏感度。
  • 激光物理。光束宽度、多回波、玻璃和高反表面都会打破“只停一次”的设定，而且一个阈值未必适合两种传感器。
  • 排序。每个高斯的响应是精确的，但射线上的先后顺序仍按逐高斯的排序键。
  • 范围。静态场景、位姿给定。运动物体、传感器仿真和位姿估计不在范围内。
  • 证据。验证从一个场地开始；跨场地、跨激光雷达型号、跨鱼眼模型的泛化还有待证明。
